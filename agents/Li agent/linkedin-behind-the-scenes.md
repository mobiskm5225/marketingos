# Skill: LinkedIn Post — Behind the Scenes

**Produces:** Build-in-public update, process reveal
**Template:** Story → Lesson → Invite
**Inherits:** `../rules/shared-content-rules.md` (length, hook length, links, hashtags, CTA, formatting)

## When to use

Content calendar Format = "Behind the Scenes", or the algorithmic rotation lands on this skill. Best source material: something actually happening in the build right now — a decision made, a mistake caught, a tool adopted, progress on the Marketing Agent OS itself. Real and current beats polished and generic.

## Inputs

- Recent build/work context (from the content calendar brief, or a recent progress-log entry if the calendar is empty)
- KB-01 Brand Voice — this skill leans more personal/informal than Educational; check voice guidance for how far that latitude goes
- KB-02 ICP — frame the lesson toward what the ICP would find useful, not just interesting to Ritwik

## Template

**Story** — Open in the moment. What was happening, what were you trying to do. Specific and concrete beats "recently, I've been thinking about..."

**Lesson** — What it taught you. One clear takeaway — not a list of three. If the story doesn't have a real lesson yet, it's not ready to post; don't manufacture one.

**Invite** — Bring the reader in rather than close the door. Per the shared CTA rule, pick one: ask what they'd have done differently, invite them to follow the build, or ask if they've hit the same problem.

## Output

- `hook`: opening line only
- `body`: full post text, story through invite, hashtags included, 150–180 words total
- `format`: "Behind the Scenes"
- `word_count`: integer
