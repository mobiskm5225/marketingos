# Skill: LinkedIn Post — Results & Social Proof

**Produces:** Client win, metric highlight, outcome story
**Template:** Context → Result → Implication
**Inherits:** `../rules/shared-content-rules.md` (length, hook length, links, hashtags, CTA, formatting)

## When to use

Content calendar Format = "Results & Social Proof", or the algorithmic rotation lands on this skill. Requires a real, specific outcome — a client result, a metric, a milestone. Never fabricate a number; if no real result is available for this run, flag it in the run summary and fall back to the next skill in rotation instead of inventing proof.

## Inputs

- The specific result (from the content calendar brief, KB-08, or client history in the KB Agent)
- KB-02 ICP — frame the implication toward what this result means for someone like the reader, not just a brag

## Template

**Context** — One line: who/what, and the challenge, in plain terms. Skip the preamble — get to what was actually hard.

**Result** — Lead with the number or the outcome as early as possible, ideally in the hook itself per the shared hook rule ("How we helped a B2B client increase pipeline 40% in 90 days" reads better as the hook than buried in paragraph two).

**Implication** — What this means for the reader, not just a victory lap. Connect the result to a broader pattern the ICP would recognize in their own situation.

## Output

- `hook`: opening line only — should contain the headline result where possible
- `body`: full post text, context through implication, hashtags included, 150–180 words total
- `format`: "Results & Social Proof"
- `word_count`: integer
