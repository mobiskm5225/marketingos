# Skill: LinkedIn Post — Educational

**Produces:** Insight-led post from a blog or KB topic
**Template:** Hook → Insight → Proof → CTA
**Inherits:** `../rules/shared-content-rules.md` (length, hook length, links, hashtags, CTA, formatting)

## When to use

Content calendar Format = "Educational", or the algorithmic rotation lands on this skill (see `prompt.md` Step 2). Best source material: a blog post, a KB-03 positioning point, or a practitioner insight from research — something with a teachable takeaway, not just an opinion (that's Founder Voice).

## Inputs

- Topic (from KB-08 Content Calendar entry, or chosen during algorithmic rotation)
- KB-01 Brand Voice — tone and vocabulary
- KB-02 ICP — who the "you" in the post is talking to
- 1–2 WebSearch results on the topic, if the topic needs current grounding

## Template

**Hook** — State the insight as a claim, not a preview. The reader should learn something from the hook alone, even before expanding.
- Bad: "Here's something I learned about LinkedIn hooks."
- Good: "Your hook isn't a preview. It's the whole pitch."

**Insight** — One paragraph (1–3 sentences) unpacking the claim. Why is it true? What's the mechanism? No throat-clearing — go straight to the reasoning.

**Proof** — One concrete example, data point, or specific scenario that makes the insight land. A number or a named specific beats a vague generality. If research/KB doesn't have a real data point, use a specific scenario instead of inventing a stat.

**CTA** — One ask per the shared CTA rule. For educational posts, a save or comment CTA ("Save this for your next post" / "What's the one hook rule you'd add?") tends to fit best — but check the CTA rotation log so it's not repeated from the last run.

## Output

- `hook`: opening line only
- `body`: full post text, hook through CTA, hashtags included, 150–180 words total
- `format`: "Educational"
- `word_count`: integer
