# Skill: LinkedIn Post — Founder Voice

**Produces:** Opinion, contrarian take, personal story
**Template:** Position → Reasoning → Call to action
**Inherits:** `../rules/shared-content-rules.md` (length, hook length, links, hashtags, CTA, formatting)

## When to use

Content calendar Format = "Founder Voice", or the algorithmic rotation lands on this skill. This is the highest-engagement, highest-risk skill — it stakes out a position. Only run it on a genuine opinion grounded in KB-03 Positioning or real experience; don't invent a contrarian take for the sake of one.

## Inputs

- KB-03 Positioning — the position taken must be consistent with established positioning, not a one-off hot take that contradicts it
- KB-01 Brand Voice — this is the most personal-register skill; use first person throughout
- KB-02 ICP — the position should matter to this audience specifically, not just be provocative in general

## Template

**Position** — State the claim plainly in the hook, per the shared hook rule. A real position has a real opposite — if nobody would disagree, it's not a position, it's a platitude.

**Reasoning** — Why you hold it. Personal experience, a pattern seen across clients/work, or a specific moment that changed your mind. This is where "founder voice" earns the name — it should be reasoning only Ritwik could have written, not generic advice.

**Call to action** — Per the shared CTA rule, this template favors a comment CTA that invites disagreement or a counter-example ("Where have you seen this fail?") — but still exactly one CTA, and rotate against recent posts.

## Output

- `hook`: opening line only
- `body`: full post text, position through CTA, hashtags included, 150–180 words total
- `format`: "Founder Voice"
- `word_count`: integer
