# TOFU Blog — Educational / "What is X"

**Source:** Ahrefs Blog — "What is SEO and Why Does It Matter for Your Website?" (ahrefs.com/blog/what-is-seo). Not a YC company. Structure and technique only, no copy reused verbatim.

## When to use

Top-of-funnel blog post for a reader who doesn't yet know they have this specific problem, or knows the symptom but not the category of solution. This is a flexible shell — use the modules that fit the specific term or trend; skip the ones that don't.

## Structure

1. **Headline** — "What is [X] and why does it matter?" or a direct definitional question. Plain language, no cleverness.
2. **Opening hook** — one-sentence, jargon-free definition, immediately followed by one concrete, relatable example that shows the stakes (not an abstract explanation).
3. **Why it matters** — a section making the consequences of ignoring [X] concrete for the reader's role.
4. **How it works** — broken into numbered sub-steps or components. This is the comprehensive core of the piece.
5. **[OPTIONAL] Specialization module** — a narrower cut of the topic relevant to a sub-audience (Ahrefs used "How local SEO works" as a niche slice of the broader SEO topic). Include only if a real sub-case exists.
6. **[OPTIONAL] Mistakes/misconceptions module** — common errors people make with [X].
7. **FAQ** — pull in the `reachify-faq-block` template for this section.

## Copy rules (from benchmark)

- Lead with the definition in the first two lines — don't bury it under scene-setting.
- Use at least one quantified claim with a cited source, and at least one visual (chart, screenshot, or diagram placeholder).
- Weave 2–4 internal links naturally into sentences (to deeper guides or adjacent topics) — never as a bare link dump.
- No product pitch. A single soft tool mention in passing is the ceiling.

## Fields to fill per article

- [ ] Term/topic being explained
- [ ] One-sentence plain-language definition
- [ ] One concrete stakes example
- [ ] Mechanism broken into numbered steps
- [ ] At least 1 stat + source
- [ ] At least 1 visual (chart/screenshot/diagram)
- [ ] 2–4 internal link opportunities
- [ ] 5–10 FAQ questions (via `reachify-faq-block`)
- [ ] Soft CTA choice (newsletter / free resource / related guide — never "book a demo")
